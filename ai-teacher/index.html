Setup (one time)

  1. Sign up for free at elevenlabs.io, open Agents and create a new agent (if it only offers templates, pick a simple one like Voice Reading Companion and replace its text). Name it "Talking Teacher".
  2. In First message, paste:
  3. In System prompt, paste:
  4. Under LLM, choose a Claude model (the newest Claude Sonnet is a good balance of smart and fast). Under Voice, pick any voice you like the sound of.
  5. Click Save / Publish. You can press Test AI agent on that page to hear the voice right away.
  6. Copy the Agent ID (it starts with agent_, shown near the agent's name or under the Widget tab) and paste it here:
  7. For the whiteboard, paste your Anthropic API key here. The app uses it to sketch what the teacher is explaining. It stays on this laptop only.
  8. Planner speed: the whiteboard asks Claude what to draw after each teacher turn. Smarter (Sonnet 5.5) plans best; Faster (Haiku 5.5) starts writing sooner.
  9. Choose how the whiteboard draws. Animated writes and draws step by step while the teacher talks and adds to what is already on the board. Classic replaces the board with a finished sketch after each turn.

The free plan includes about 15 minutes of talking per month. That's enough to try it out. Use headphones or speakers, either works; this one handles echo for you.
